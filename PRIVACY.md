# Privacy — Kunle Watchlist Sync

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Kunle Watchlist Sync is a personal integration operated by a single individual (Richard Bankole, Trakt: @rickoslyder) for his own use only. It has no other users and is not offered to the public as a service.

## Data accessed

- The integration accesses only the operator's own Trakt account data: his watchlist and watch history.
- It does not access, collect or store any other person's data.

## Data use and sharing

- Data is used solely to keep the operator's own local watchlist file and viewing diary in sync with his Trakt account.
- No data is sold, shared, rented or disclosed to any third party. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

## Storage and security

- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a secure credential vault. The app uses PKCE and has no Client Secret embedded anywhere.
- Synced watchlist data is stored in the operator's own private files.

## Revocation and deletion

- The operator can revoke the app's access at any time from his Trakt account settings, and can delete the locally stored data at any time.
